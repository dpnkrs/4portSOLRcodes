E2. Wave recording during every reciprocal-thru measurement (max-safety; include this)

For every reciprocal-thru dataset in Section G (straight + orthogonal; arc + diagonal where applicable):

Save S-parameters

Also save the receiver waves(𝑎𝑖,𝑏𝑖,𝑎𝑗,𝑏𝑗) for the active pair under each stimulus direction

Operationally:

When stimulating port 𝑖 record at least(𝑎𝑗,𝑏𝑗) (receiver at 𝑗)
When stimulating port 𝑗, record at least (𝑎𝑖,𝑏𝑖)) (receiver at 𝑖)
This allows computing switch ratios for that dataset:

Γ𝑖→𝑗(𝑓)∝𝑏𝑗(𝑓)/𝑎𝑗(𝑓),
Γ𝑗→𝑖(𝑓)∝𝑏𝑖(𝑓)/𝑎𝑖(𝑓)
